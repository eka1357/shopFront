## Booking, Rescheduling & Cancellation Policies

### Booking Windows & Advance Notice
- Appointments can be booked up to 60 days in advance.
- Walk-ins are accepted if a stylist is free, but advance bookings are strongly recommended.

### 24-Hour Cancellation & Reschedule Policy
- We require at least **24 hours advance notice** to cancel or reschedule an appointment without penalty.
- **Late Cancellations (less than 24 hours notice)**: Incur a cancellation fee equal to 50% of the scheduled service price.
- **Missed Appointments / No-Shows**: Charged 100% of the scheduled service price.

### Late Arrival Policy
- We provide a **15-minute grace period**.
- If a client arrives more than 15 minutes late, we may need to modify the scheduled service to ensure subsequent clients are not delayed, or reschedule the appointment with a late fee.

### Refund & Adjustment Policy
- **No Monetary Refunds**: We do not issue cash, check, or credit card refunds on completed salon services.
- **48-Hour Complimentary Adjustment**: If a client is unsatisfied with their cut or color, they must contact the salon within **48 hours** of their service. We will gladly schedule a complimentary adjustment appointment with Sarah or the original stylist within 7 business days.
- **Retail Products**: Unopened hair care products may be returned within 14 days for salon credit or exchange with receipt.
